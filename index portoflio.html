About

I'm an emergency medical professional, biomedical science student, researcher, and technology developer focused on the intersection of medicine and artificial intelligence.

My background combines prehospital emergency care, clinical medicine, biomedical science, scientific writing, research, fitness, and software development.

A major part of my work is exploring how emerging technology can be translated into practical healthcare education and research tools.

Through independent projects and EMARI, I develop prototypes involving machine learning, large language models, clinical information systems, and healthcare education.
